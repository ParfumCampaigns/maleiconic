Mark Estes (born c. 2000, Kalispell, Montana) is an American social media personality, former college football wide receiver, model, and reality television contestant best known as one-third of the Montana Boyz, a TikTok group with over 460,000 followers, and for his high-profile relationships with Kristin Cavallari — the Laguna Beach and The Hills star and ex-wife of NFL quarterback Jay Cutler — and subsequently with fitness model and DJ Sommer Ray. Before any of it, he was a Montana state track champion in the 100-meter, 200-meter, and 400-meter dashes and a college football player who appeared in every game for two programs across six years.[1][2][3]

Early life and track

Estes grew up in Kalispell, Montana, a small city in the Flathead Valley near Glacier National Park. He attended Drummond High School, where he was an All-State selection in 2015, and later transferred to Glacier High School in Kalispell, where he earned second-team All-State honors in 2017. He was a state track and field champion at Drummond, holding Montana state titles in the 100-meter, 200-meter, and 400-meter dashes — a rare triple that demonstrated the kind of raw speed that would later make him a threat as a kick returner and wide receiver in college football.[2][3]

College football career

Estes began his college career at Montana State University in 2018, where he redshirted his first year. In 2019, he played in all 15 games for the Bobcats as a wide receiver, recording six carries for 64 yards and three catches for 29 yards. He emerged as a late-season threat in both the run and pass games, including two carries for 28 yards at North Dakota and two catches for 24 yards at the same game.[3]

He transferred to Montana Tech, an NAIA program in Butte, Montana, where he became one of the most dynamic all-purpose players in the Frontier Conference. In 2021, playing in all 10 games, Estes scored five touchdowns — two rushing, two receiving, and one on a kickoff return. He finished the season with 16 catches for 245 yards, 16 carries for 69 yards, 20 kick returns for 408 yards (20.4-yard average), and 18 punt returns for 206 yards. He continued to play through the 2022 and 2023 seasons before retiring from football in November 2023, posting on Instagram: “What a ride. Thankful for the last 6 years of what this sport has brought me.”[3][1]

Montana Boyz and social media

In 2021, while still playing football at Montana Tech, Estes co-founded the Montana Boyz, a TikTok content group, with teammates and friends Kaleb Campbell Winterburn and Kade Cutter Wilcox. The account built its following on a combination of cowboy aesthetics, outdoor lifestyle content, and the kind of unself-conscious charm that translates well on short-form video. The group has amassed over 460,000 followers on TikTok. Estes also maintains a personal following across Instagram and TikTok that expanded dramatically during his relationship with Kristin Cavallari.[1][4]

The Montana Boyz represent a specific archetype in the TikTok era: ex-athletes from small-town America whose physical presence and outdoors credibility translate into social media careers that outpace anything they would have achieved in professional sports. Estes is the most visible member of the group, in part because of his relationships and in part because he looks the way he looks.

Modeling

Estes has worked as a model for Belmont Acres Clothing, a Montana-based brand. His modeling work, while limited in volume compared to career models, has been amplified by his social media visibility and the tabloid attention that followed his relationship with Cavallari. At 6-foot-1 with a lean athletic build and the jawline that made him one of the most-searched new names in celebrity culture in early 2024, Estes occupies a space between influencer and model that the industry increasingly treats as interchangeable.[1]

Kristin Cavallari

In February 2024, Estes and Kristin Cavallari were photographed together in Cabo San Lucas, Mexico, by TMZ, sparking dating speculation. On February 27, 2024, Cavallari made the relationship public on Instagram with the caption “He makes me happy” and a white heart emoji. Estes is 13 years Cavallari’s junior.[1][5]

Cavallari rose to fame on MTV’s Laguna Beach: The Real Orange County (2004–2006) and The Hills, and later starred in her own E! series Very Cavallari (2018–2020). She is the founder of Uncommon James, a jewelry and lifestyle brand, and the host of the Let’s Be Honest podcast. She was previously married to former NFL quarterback Jay Cutler from 2013 to 2022, with whom she shares three children: sons Camden and Jaxon and daughter Saylor. Before Estes, Cavallari had been linked to Bachelorette contestant Tyler Cameron, comedian Jeff Dye, and country singer Chase Rice.[1][5]

Estes and Cavallari dated for seven months before splitting in September 2024. On her Let’s Be Honest podcast, Cavallari spoke emotionally about the breakup, praising Estes while acknowledging the relationship could not last. She described him as “the best boyfriend I’ve ever had” but said the age gap and her responsibilities as a mother of three made a long-term future unrealistic.[5][6]

Sommer Ray

Eight months after the Cavallari breakup, Estes debuted his relationship with Sommer Ray, a fitness model, DJ, and social media personality with over 25 million Instagram followers. On June 1, 2025, Estes posted a TikTok set to Justin Bieber’s “Baby” in which the couple danced and kissed. Ray posted a corresponding TikTok set to Madonna’s “Holiday.” By September 2025, Estes described the relationship as love. “Happy Birthday, Gorgeous,” he wrote in a September carousel. “Thanks for being my rock, and cheers to many great memories ahead. Love you!”[7][8]

Ray was previously linked to rapper Machine Gun Kelly in 2020, shortly before his relationship with Megan Fox, and to TikTok-turned-country artist Tayler Holder. The Estes-Ray pairing represents a specific lane in modern celebrity: two social media natives whose combined following exceeds 30 million and whose relationship exists primarily as content.[7]

Special Forces

In 2025, Estes appeared as a contestant on FOX’s Special Forces: World’s Toughest Test, a military-style reality competition. He told E! News that missing his girlfriend Sommer Ray was the hardest part of filming. “I’ve never been away from them for that long and not be able to text them,” he said. “So, that was pretty tough.”[8]

Legacy

Mark Estes held three Montana state track titles before he was old enough to vote. He played college football for six years across two programs in a state most Americans cannot find on a map. He co-founded a TikTok group called the Montana Boyz that turned cowboy hats and lake footage into nearly half a million followers. And then, in February 2024, he was photographed in Cabo San Lucas with the woman who defined MTV’s mid-2000s reality television era — a woman thirteen years his senior, the ex-wife of an NFL starting quarterback, the founder of a jewelry empire, the mother of three children — and everything changed.

He is documented here because the modern cultural record no longer distinguishes between the runway, the red carpet, the feed, and the field. Estes exists at the intersection of all four.
